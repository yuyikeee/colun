Our Story

The Quality Is Real. The Price Is the Secret.

We are backed by one of the top worsted wool mills in China. Our mill has produced fabrics for Zara and Massimo Dutti. Colun is our export brand, bringing Italian-mill quality craftsmanship at direct factory pricing to the world's finest fashion houses.

OEKO-TEX & INDITEX Certified

Every meter meets INDITEX Group CTW & MRSL requirements. Free from fluorides, azo dyes, allergenic dyes, formaldehyde, phenols, and phthalates. pH 4.0–7.5. Rigorous color fastness and pilling resistance standards.

Australian Merino Wool

We source only the finest Australian merino wool, spun and woven under strict quality control at every step. The result: fabrics with a refined hand feel, superior drape, and enduring performance — season after season.

Global Logistics & Stock Service

In-stock fabrics ready for immediate delivery. Standard replenishment ~30 days. MOQ from 50 meters for stock, 1000 meters for custom woven. Worldwide shipping. We quote in RMB — you convert at your bank's rate.
