About Colun

Crafting Fabrics That Define Quality

Colun is a premier manufacturer of worsted wool fabrics, supplying leading international fashion brands with materials that meet the most stringent quality and sustainability standards in the industry.

Certified Quality

Every meter of fabric — free from fluorides, azo dyes, allergenic dyes, formaldehyde, phenols, and phthalates.

Sustainable Production

We source the finest Australian merino wool, working with ethically certified mills to ensure every step of our supply chain meets global environmental standards.

Global Supply Chain

Reliable bulk production with consistent quality. MOQ from 50 meters per batch. Trusted partner for fashion brands across Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

Our Collections

Premium Fabric Series

Seven distinct collections designed to meet the diverse needs of luxury fashion brands — from classic worsted wool to innovative natural fiber blends. Each collection carries the mark of one of the Five Elements (五行 Wuxing) — Wood, Water, Metal, Earth, and Fire — an ancient Chinese philosophy of balance and harmony.
