Series Overview

Tempest is a groundbreaking functional fabric featuring wrinkle-resistance and bidirectional natural stretch. Made from 100% Australian Merino superfine wool (Super 110s, 18.0 micron average diameter), weighing 380 g/m with 145 cm width, this worsted fabric employs a proprietary four-ply high-twist yarn structure.

This special tight yarn construction — more compact and crisp than traditional two-ply high-twist yarns — dramatically improves wrinkle resistance, strength, drape, durability, and anti-pilling performance. Most notably, we've achieved bidirectional natural stretch functionality in pure natural fiber, a domestic first.

Innovation Highlight: Through specialized equipment and process adjustments, this fabric achieves 8-10% stretch in both warp and weft directions without adding chemical elastic fibers. The elastic recovery performance is excellent, meeting market demand for stretch in both jackets and trousers.

Fabric Characteristics

The high-twist yarn structure gives the fabric a pronounced grainy texture with finer detail. The hand-feel is firm and crisp with elasticity while maintaining toughness. The fabric is substantial yet breathable, with a soft matte luster suitable for all seasons — especially ideal for business daily wear requiring frequent wearing.

Summary: This product contains no synthetic fibers yet achieves four-way stretch, making it very friendly for joint movement and all-around activity. Combined with the four-ply yarn structure, its overall firmness and wrinkle resistance are standout advantages.

Technical Specifications

Composition: 100% Virgin Wool — Super 110s Australian Merino
Weight: 380 g/m
Width: 145 cm
Yarn Structure: 4-ply high-twist
Stretch: 8-10% natural bidirectional stretch

Applications

Ideal for business suits, travel blazers, and daily commuter wear. The natural stretch and wrinkle resistance make this fabric perfect for professionals who need garments that perform through long days and frequent movement.
