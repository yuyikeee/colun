Series Overview

Solstice is Colun's flagship collection, encompassing four distinct sub-series that showcase the finest qualities of merino wool tailoring. Each sub-collection offers refined fabric character and quality, expressing merino wool's excellent characteristics: breathable natural fiber, stronger than cashmere, with silk's softness and smoothness, and more elastic than cotton.

The weave constructions use 2/2 twill and variations, with colorways and arrangements creating rich yet refined fabric backgrounds. Color treatments include solids, heathers, warp-weft contrast, slub yarns, and top-dyed effects. Patterns include timeless serge, multiple stripe variations, pinpoint, herringbone, sharkskin, cavalry twill, houndstooth, windowpane, glen plaid, and Prince of Wales check, plus texture-rich miniature designs.

Applications span all-season business suiting, luxury formal wear, evening attire, commuter wear, and professional uniforms.

The Four Sub-Collections

2501A/C

Super 110s Wool Excellence — Classic Suiting

What distinguishes our Super 110s wool is the finishing for wrinkle resistance and crispness. The fabric conveys a smooth, firm, crisp hand-feel, exhibiting excellent natural elasticity and good elastic recovery without any chemical additives. Wrinkle resistance is outstanding. The flexible yet structured quality and good drape perfectly express this series' character. Garments demonstrate excellent comfort and shape retention.

2501B/D

Super 110s Wool Flannel

The classic flannel series transforms into exceptionally refined fabric when made with superfine wool. Hand-feel is full, soft, elastic, with gentle luster and pure color. The series offers three nap levels:

  • Light Nap: Subtle nap with visible weave. Soft luster, cashmere-like hand.
  • Medium Nap: Dense nap with faint weave. Compact structure with body, matte surface, premium feel.
  • Full Nap: Very dense nap, obscured weave, similar to woolen flannel. Compact, wrinkle-resistant, wind-blocking, warm, timeless classic.
2502A/B

Super 130s Wool Cashmere — Luxury Blend (客户推荐首选)

This series exhibits a soft, smooth cashmere hand-feel — a softness and elasticity that brings comfort and pleasure, the primary characteristics of cashmere fiber. The product fully leverages the advantages of cashmere and superfine wool: refined weave and flexible texture, natural stretch, elongation, elastic recovery, drape and wrinkle resistance, moisture wicking, breathability, non-irritating — excellent characteristics throughout.

Quality and performance reach world-class levels, suitable for all-season luxury suiting.

2503A/B

Super 150s Wool Superfine — Ultimate Luxury

Products made from Australia's precious Super 150s Merino Wool are extremely limited globally in high-end worsted fabrics, with legendary reputation and regarded as luxury fabric. Ultra-fine wool is chosen to achieve extremely refined weave texture, crease recovery, and pleasurable hand-feel. The fabric feels exceptionally smooth and silky. When touching skin, it conveys a sensation of pleasure. The fabric's natural elasticity provides wearing comfort. The smooth, refined, matte appearance presents nobility and elegance.

This series uses double-warp double-weft yarn structure, enhancing fabric durability and wear resistance.
