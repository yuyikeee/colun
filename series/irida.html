Series Overview

Irida represents a return to fundamental values in fabric manufacturing. This wool-polyester blend collection combines classic construction with modern Italian finishing technology to deliver professional suiting fabrics that balance performance, aesthetics, and value.

The name "Irida" (霓裳) evokes the image of radiant robes catching light — a fitting metaphor for fabrics that achieve refined elegance through honest materials and meticulous craftsmanship rather than artificial enhancement.

Key Features

  • Biella-Finish Italian Craftsmanship: Proprietary Italian BIELLA-FINISH post-processing technology recreates the refined texture of Italian suiting fabrics — crisp, resilient, wrinkle-resistant, and durable.
  • Superior Comfort & Stability: Soft silky touch with exceptional draping. Breathable and non-stuffy with superior moisture-wicking performance — far exceeding standard polyester blends in comfort.
  • Elegant Matte Luster: Fine-denier fibers create natural light diffusion, producing a refined matte sheen — no harsh plastic shine typical of ordinary polyester. Understated and sophisticated.
  • Easy Care & Durability: Machine-washable with excellent color fastness. No fading, no deformation after washing. Wrinkle-resistant for effortless daily care. Passes ironing tests with ease.
  • PPM<5 Softened Water Weaving: Entire production process uses softened water (PPM<5), achieving saturated, soft coloration. Advanced finishing technology balances structure with a gentle hand feel — avoiding the stiff, boardy texture of conventional wool-polyester fabrics.

The Biella-Finish Process

Named after Biella, Italy — the historic heart of fine wool finishing — the BIELLA-FINISH process applies traditional Italian textile engineering principles to modern worsted blends. This proprietary finishing sequence emphasizes three core qualities:

  • Crispness without stiffness: The fabric holds its shape and resists wrinkles while maintaining fluid drape and comfortable hand-feel.
  • Resilience through structure: High-count yarns and balanced weave construction provide durability that outlasts conventional blends.
  • Refined surface character: Careful finishing creates a matte luster that reads as natural rather than synthetic — the hallmark of quality suiting.

Material Philosophy

Irida embraces the wool-polyester blend not as a compromise but as a deliberate choice. When properly engineered, this combination delivers practical advantages that pure wool cannot match: enhanced durability, easier care, consistent performance across seasons, and accessible pricing without sacrificing professional appearance.

The softened-water weaving process (PPM<5) ensures that dyes penetrate evenly and deeply, producing colors that remain saturated through years of wear. This attention to fundamental manufacturing details — often overlooked in mass-market fabrics — is what separates honest craft from mere cost reduction.

Applications

Ideal for business suiting, corporate uniforms, professional wear programs, and ready-to-wear collections where consistent quality, easy care, and value pricing are essential. The machine-washable finish and wrinkle resistance make this series particularly well-suited for frequent travelers and professionals who need garments that perform reliably without constant maintenance.
