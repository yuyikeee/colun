Series Overview

Elyra is an all-wool four-way natural stretch (Bi-Stretch) worsted fabric with bidirectional elasticity and wrinkle-resistant functionality. Using Australian Merino superfine wool with a special tight yarn structure and combination — more compact and crisp than traditional two-ply high-twist yarns — the fabric undergoes specialized finishing with squeeze-type pre-shrinking technology to provide exceptional elasticity, excellent wrinkle resistance and drape, plus high strength and durability.

Notably, without adding chemical elastic fibers, the fabric achieves natural bidirectional stretch.

Performance Specifications

  • Warp Stretch: 8-12%
  • Weft Stretch: 10-15%
  • Wrinkle Resistance: Grade 4
  • Abrasion Resistance: 10,000+ cycles

Outstanding Features

  • Comfort & Mobility: Core technology allows 2D fabric to perfectly shape 3D garments. Bidirectional stretch provides unparalleled freedom of movement and comfort — ideal for business travel, outdoor leisure, daily commuting, and professionals with frequent activity or extended sitting.
  • Crisp Shape & Excellent Drape: Worsted high-twist yarn and segmented arrangement of multi-ply 3-ply yarns combined with warp-weft density ratios create fine surface texture. The fabric has crisp hand and tension, allowing suits to be sharp, fitted, comfortable, with flowing lines.
  • Wrinkle Resistance & Shape Retention: The "3-ply + high-twist" bidirectional natural stretch fabric achieves excellent elasticity, recovery, and wrinkle resistance through yarn structure, fabric parameters, and special finishing — wrinkles smooth quickly even after prolonged sitting.
  • Four-Season & Easy Care: Special yarn structure and parameters make the fabric breathable and cool, while being more abrasion-resistant, durable, and long-lasting. The appearance and weight make it perfect for all seasons, emphasizing free movement, crisp wrinkle resistance, and lasting durability — an excellent choice for modern business professionals.

Applications

Ideal for business suits, travel blazers, sport coats, and modern tailoring. The natural stretch and wrinkle resistance make this fabric perfect for professionals who need garments that perform through long days and frequent movement.
