Series Overview

The Perennial series represents high-performance, classic-style suiting fabrics. Blending superfine merino wool with polyester fiber and spun with high-count yarns, this collection offers diverse weaves and styles including warp stretch, four-way stretch, and jet-black finishes.

Polyester is a globally popular functional fiber. When combined with wool fiber, it merges the advantages of superfine merino wool — soft, smooth, naturally crimped, elastic, and comfortable to wear — while delivering competitive pricing that achieves the hand-feel and weight typically found only in expensive high-count pure wool fabrics.

Outstanding Features

  • Ultra-fine polyester fibers are exceptionally slender — finer than silk — enabling high-count yarn production when blended with wool.
  • The fiber's soft luster gives the fabric a luxurious hand and appearance; the two fibers integrate well, complementing each other's strengths.
  • Polyester provides superior wearing performance, ensuring comfort and compensating for wool's lower tensile strength.
  • The blended fabric cuts smoothly, maintains shape well, has good sewing performance, and is more durable.
  • Wrinkle-resistant and shape-retentive — garments hold their form and are easy to care for.

Applications

Ideal for business suits, blazers, trousers, and uniforms. The wool-polyester blend delivers professional performance at accessible price points, making it perfect for ready-to-wear brands seeking quality and value.
